About Me

Hello! I'm Pragati Bagri, a Fashion Communication student with a strong interest in visual storytelling, fashion, branding and creative direction.

I enjoy exploring the connection between fashion, culture and communication. My work ranges from editorial design and brand research to campaigns, visual identities and creative concepts.

I believe good design is not only about making something look beautiful. It is about creating a visual language that communicates an idea, connects with people and leaves an impression.
